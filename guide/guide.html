Creature Pals

https://nicklevasseur.github.io/creature-pals

Created by Nick Levasseur

Creature Pals is a 3D toy with fifteen plush animal friends. Kids pick a friend, make it do tricks, spin it around and give it a name. Once it is on the home screen it runs like an app, with no ads, no accounts and no internet needed after the first visit.

Put it on the home screen

Android (Chrome)
  1. Open Chrome and go to the link at the top of this page.
  2. Wait for the title screen to appear.
  3. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  4. Tap Add to Home screen (some phones say Install app).
  5. Tap Install or Add.
  6. Open Creature Pals from the otter icon on the home screen.
iPhone and iPad (Safari)
  1. Open Safari and go to the link at the top of this page.
  2. Wait for the title screen to appear.
  3. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  4. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add.
  6. Open Creature Pals from the otter icon on the home screen.
Playing with no internet: open the app once while connected and let it load. After that it works anywhere, even in airplane mode. When there is a new version, it updates the next time the app opens with internet.
No sound on an iPhone? Turn off silent mode (the switch or Action button on the side) and turn the volume up.

How to play

  • Tap to play on the title screen starts the game and the sounds.
  • Choose a friend from the menu at the top: possum, giraffe, gorilla, otter, sloth, monkey, chicken, cow, pigeon, tarantula, octopus, crab, lobster, manta ray and whale.
  • Trick buttons under each friend make it move and make noise. Every friend has its own tricks, like the gorilla's chest pound or the whale's breach.
  • Drag with one finger to spin the friend around. Pinch to zoom in and out.
  • Tap the friend to say hi.
  • Color dots change the friend's fur color.
  • Name me lets you type a name for the friend and tap Save, or tap Surprise me for a silly one. Each friend remembers its name.
  • Sound on / off at the top turns the sounds and the habitat noises on or off.
Animal sounds are real recordings from NOAA, the National Park Service and free sound libraries. The full list is in the app's sounds folder (CREDITS.md).
